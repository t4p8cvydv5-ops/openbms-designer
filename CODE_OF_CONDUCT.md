# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in OpenBMS Designer a respectful,
welcoming and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus technical discussion on evidence and engineering assumptions.
- Accept feedback and correct mistakes transparently.
- Protect confidential project and customer information.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate disclosure
of private information, and sustained disruption are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these
standards and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report a
problem privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile.
